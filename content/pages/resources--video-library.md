## Learn your numbers on your own schedule

Not every question needs a phone call. Sometimes you just need two minutes and a straight answer, whether that's how to upload a W-2 into your portal or why the excess fertility deduction might apply to your operation this year. The TruCount video library was built for exactly that: short, plain-English videos you can watch between chores or after the kids are in bed. No jargon, no sales pitch, just the same answers Julie Underwood, CPA, and the team would give you across the table. Watch what you need, skip what you don't, and get back to running your operation.

### The R&D credit most farmers never claim

### Understanding the excess fertility deduction

## Frequently Asked Questions About Video library

**Q: Are the videos in the TruCount video library free to watch?**
