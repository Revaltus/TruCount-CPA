## Join our team

TruCount CPA PC opened its doors in Brookings, South Dakota in 2009, starting as a one-person practice before growing into the firm it is today. Kristy and Brandi have each spent 13 years here, and together they bring 51 years of accounting experience to farmers, agribusiness operators, and professional service business owners across the Midwest.

We're looking for people who want more than a seat at a desk. Our team gets to know clients personally, not just their tax returns, and that kind of work takes people who are curious, steady under a deadline, and genuinely interested in helping someone else's business run better. If that sounds like how you want to spend your career, we'd like to hear from you.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: accounting-team-office.jpg | alt: "Accounting team members collaborating around a laptop in a modern office" | query: "accounting team collaborating office" -->
## Why work with us

Public accounting has a reputation for burnout, especially during tax season. We've built something different: a fixed-fee, bundled service model that keeps workload predictable year-round instead of concentrated into a few brutal months. That predictability shows up in how we staff and how we treat people.

- Real mentorship from CPAs like Julie Underwood, who works directly with staff on multi-entity agribusiness tax strategy and succession planning
- Competitive pay benchmarked against South Dakota Society of CPAs data
- Flexibility to manage your schedule outside the narrowest weeks of the year
- A clear path to more responsibility as you grow, not a ceiling you hit early
- Access to the same secure online platform our clients use, so you're never digging through email for a document

## Our hiring process

### Submit your application
Send a resume and a short note about what you're looking for. We read every one that comes in.

### Initial conversation
A 30-minute call to talk about the role, your experience, and whether Brookings and TruCount fit where you want your career to go.

### Meet the team
An interview, in person or by video, with the people you'd actually work alongside, not just a recruiter.

### Offer and onboarding
If it's a match, we move quickly. You'll get a clear start date, a real training plan, and access to our systems before day one.

**Q: Does TruCount CPA PC offer remote or hybrid roles?**
A: Most roles are based in our Brookings, South Dakota office, since much of the work involves in-person collaboration with farm and business clients. That said, some positions offer hybrid flexibility, such as remote work one or two days a week once training is complete, depending on the role.

**Q: How do I apply for a job at TruCount CPA PC?**
A: Send your resume and a short note about the type of work you're looking for through our contact page. Mention a specific opening, like the Office Coordinator role, if one fits, or let us know generally what interests you. We personally read every application and follow up within about a week.

**Q: What qualifications do I need for a staff accountant role?**
A: We look for accuracy, curiosity, and comfort working directly with clients more than a specific number of years on the job. A degree in accounting or a related field helps, and experience with QuickBooks or similar software is useful, but we've trained strong candidates from related backgrounds too.

**Q: Does TruCount CPA PC hire seasonally for tax season?**
A: Occasionally, particularly for tax preparation support between January and April. Because our fixed-fee, bundled service model spreads work more evenly across the year than a typical CPA firm, seasonal hiring is limited, but we do bring on temporary help during the busiest weeks when needed.
