## Agriculture and agribusiness specific resources

Farm and agribusiness clients carry document needs that a generic tax checklist skips entirely. If you're testing new seed varieties, cover crop mixes, or precision ag equipment, keep records supporting the R&D credit, including trial notes, invoices, and time logs tied to the experimentation. If you've applied lime, gypsum, or other soil amendments to bring fertility up on newly purchased ground, hold onto soil test results and the closing statement, since those support an excess fertility deduction. Our [agriculture](/industries/agriculture) practice keeps a running list of what qualifies and what documentation the IRS actually wants to see, so you're not reconstructing records from memory two years later.

## Helpful external links and state resources

Some forms don't originate with us, and there's no reason to pretend otherwise. For federal filings, the [IRS forms and publications page](https://www.irs.gov/forms-instructions) is the source we point clients to directly. South Dakota entity paperwork, including Articles of Organization and annual reports, runs through the [SD Secretary of State's business portal](https://sosenterprise.sd.gov). Sales tax and use tax questions route to the [SD Department of Revenue](https://dor.sd.gov). If you're weighing a SEP-IRA against a Simple IRA or a 401(k) for your team, our [retirement plan analysis](/services/retirement-plan-analysis) service walks through the paperwork before you commit to a plan structure that costs more than it should.
