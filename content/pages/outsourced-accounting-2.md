**Seasonal and agricultural labor.** Farms and agribusinesses often bring on extra hands for planting, spraying, and harvest, then scale back when the season ends. We set up new seasonal employees quickly, keep track of hours, process payroll on your schedule, and make the offseason adjustments cleanly. You get accurate paychecks without having to rebuild the process every spring.

**Multi-state service crews.** If your professional service or field team works across state lines, payroll can get complicated fast. We help keep the details organized so your crews are paid correctly and your filings stay on track, wherever the work takes them.

**Professional service teams.** When your staff is made up of salaried and hourly employees, you need reliable processing with clear records. We take care of it every pay period so payroll never competes with client work for your attention.

Here is what our payroll service covers:

- Processing scheduled payrolls on time, every time
- Employee setup and salary changes

Tax work should not be a once a year scramble. When tax planning is part of your monthly relationship, you can make decisions with the tax impact in mind before the year ends, not after. Our business tax service pairs tax preparation with personalized, year-round advice, all delivered through a secure digital process that makes document collection simple.

We handle tax document collection, deduction analysis, and electronic filing for your business, and we keep the conversation going between filings. Because we already handle your books, we are working from current, accurate numbers. That makes it much easier to find savings, avoid surprises, and plan ahead. **Tax strategy for farms and agribusinesses.** Agriculture has its own tax landscape, and it pays to work with someone who knows it well. We help farm clients look at strategies that can make a real difference, including:

- Income averaging and deferred grain contract tax strategy, which can help you manage the ups and downs of farm income from year to year
